## Cam · `Fandom Wiki (en)`
- **来源**: https://squishmallowsquad.fandom.com/wiki/Cam

**简介**：Cam the Cat, full name Cameron, is a white Squishmallow with black and brown patches. Cam the Cat was one of the first eight Squishmallows to ever be released. Cam is listed as the first ever Squishmallow by Collector Number. Cam the Cat serves as the main mascot of the Squishmallows© brand.

**外貌与造型描述**：Cam is a calico cat, meaning he has a cream body and brown and black patches. He has one brown patch over his right ear, and two black patches, one on his left ear and the other on his lower left body. He has closed eyes, black whiskers, and a happy, pink cat mouth. The Laying Hug Mee Cam has the colors of his ears and face spots swapped for an unknown reason.

## Squishmallows · `English Wikipedia`
- **来源**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squishmallows

**简介**：Squishmallows is a brand of stuffed toy that was launched in 2017 by Kelly Toys Holdings LLC and is owned by Jazwares, a Berkshire Hathaway company. Squishmallows are round and come in a variety of colors, sizes, animals/foods/objects, and textures. The brand has created over 3,000 Squishmallow designs.
