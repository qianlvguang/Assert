**简介**：《汪汪队立大功》（英语：PAW Patrol）是加拿大电脑成像儿童动画影集，由基思·查普曼创作，斯平玛斯特制作。内容叙述由一位热心、精通科技、聪明，名叫莱德的11岁小孩带领的小狗巡逻队，共同保护一个名为冒险湾的海岸城市的故事。 斯平玛斯特为该动画建立衍生系列产品，其中包括多款周边玩具。《汪汪队立大功》玩具销售为公司创造了数百万美元的收入，并增加了斯平玛斯特在学前玩具市场的影响力。该动画还获得加拿大影视学院和电视艺术与科学学院的多种奖项。改编院线电影于2021年上映。台湾由 MyVideo上架播出。

## Paw Patrol · `English Wikipedia`
- **来源**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paw_Patrol

**简介**：Paw Patrol is a Canadian children's animated television series created by Keith Chapman and produced by Spin Master Entertainment, with animation provided by Guru Studio. In Canada, the series is primarily broadcast on TVOntario as part of the TVOKids programing block. TVO first ran previews of the show in August 2013. The series premiered on Nickelodeon in the United States on August 12, 2013. The series focuses on a young boy named Ryder who leads a crew of police dogs, firehouse dogs, and search and rescue dogs that call themselves the Paw Patrol. They work together on missions to protect the shoreside community of Adventure Bay and surrounding areas. Each dog has a specific set of skills based on emergency services professions, such as a firefighter, a construction worker, a police officer, a recycler, a maritime search and rescuer, and an aircraft pilot. They reside in doghouses that transform into customized vehicles or "pupmobiles" for their missions. They are equipped with special hi-tech backpacks called "pup packs" that contain tools relating to their respective job.
