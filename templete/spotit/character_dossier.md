## Dobble · `English Wikipedia`
- **来源**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dobble

**简介**：Dobble is a game in which players have to find symbols in common between two cards. It was the UK’s best-selling game in 2018 and 2019. The game is sold as Dobble in Europe and Spot It! in the US. The name is a play on the word 'double'.
