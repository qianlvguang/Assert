**外貌与造型描述**：在原作漫画中，三笠的人物形象更偏向冷感理智。而在WIT STUDIO改编的前三季动画中，有些过于刻画三笠对艾伦的执着，同时删减了其不少的心理描写和反思，从而使人物形象显得有些扁平以至于让一部分观众为后来三笠阻止艾伦地鸣的决定感到困惑。

**补充特征/能力**：《进击的巨人》女主角。与摩尔迦娜、常守朱并称ACG界的三大纯爷们。835年出生，845年初次登场。 沉默寡言、表情稀少的美少女，拥有东洋与阿克曼的血统。拥有八块腹肌的女汉子，在漫画第32话暴露了自己的腹肌 艾伦和阿明的青梅竹马与挚友，三人经常一起行动。 拥有优秀的身体能力与战斗技术，一人的表现就足以与100名士兵匹敌（15岁时）。为了保护艾伦，加入调查兵团。 第104期训练兵团毕业生首席，被教官评价为史无前例的天才。因为幼年时失去双亲，十分重视家人。平时沉着冷静，但是艾伦陷入危险时容易失去理智。

## List of Attack on Titan characters · `English Wikipedia`
- **来源**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Attack_on_Titan_characters

**简介**：The Attack on Titan manga series features an extensive cast of fictional characters created by Hajime Isayama. The story is set in a world where humanity lives in cities surrounded by enormous walls; a defense against the Titans, gigantic humanoids that eat humans seemingly without reason. The story initially centers on Eren Yeager with his childhood friends Mikasa Ackerman and Armin Arlert who join the defense force to fight the Titans after their home town is invaded and Eren's mother is eaten. They are part of the 104th Training Corps, whose graduates assume different positions in the Military, including the Garrison Regiment, the Survey Corps and the Military Police Brigade. It is later revealed that the area where the Walls are located is an island called Paradis (パラディ, Paradi) and that it is the last territory of Eldia (エルディア, Erudia). There are other nations outside the island, namely Marley (マーレ, Māre), a nation on the mainland which declared war on Paradis.
