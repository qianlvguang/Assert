## 哥斯拉 · `中文维基百科`
- **来源**: https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%93%A5%E6%96%AF%E6%8B%89

**简介**：哥吉拉（日语：ゴジラ，罗马化：Gojira，英语：Godzilla、/ɡɒdˈzɪlə/; [ɡoꜜ(d)ʑiɾa] ），为日本东宝株式会社《哥斯拉系列》所登场的怪兽，是日本影史上最悠久，世界影史上公认最经典最有名的怪兽角色，更是日本流行文化中最知名的象征之一。 哥吉拉的背景与成功多半被视为日本人对战争记忆，及对原子能爱恨交加的矛盾情绪的体会。在设定中，哥斯拉是一个巨大、具有破坏性的史前巨兽，在被辐射污染的海域中复活后，开始在日本本土展开破坏城市等巨大灾害。背后则影射著广岛与长崎原子弹爆炸以及第五福龙丸等核子试验造成的污染，因此该生物也曾被视为核武器的隐喻。 哥吉拉于1954年首次亮相，总共主演了38部电影，随著电影系列的扩展，以及导入日本社会上的流行与话题性事物，包括日本对其大日本帝国历史的忽视、自然灾害和人类社会，哥吉拉在银幕上的性格与形象也开始改变，并描绘成反英雄或捍卫人类的守护者。并与拉顿、摩斯拉誉为“东宝三大怪兽”。更成为当代日本影视文化电影、动画、漫画的怪兽原型，对于日本动漫作品有著非常深远的影响，同时不仅在日本，哥斯拉在世界其他国家也极受欢迎。

## Godzilla · `English Wikipedia`
- **来源**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godzilla

**简介**：Godzilla ( ɡod-ZIL-ə) is a fictional giant monster, or daikaijū, that first appeared in the 1954 film of the same name, directed by Ishirō Honda. The character has since become an international pop culture icon, appearing in various media: 33 Japanese films by Toho, five American films, and numerous video games, novels, comic books, and television shows. Godzilla has been dubbed the King of the Monsters, an epithet first used in Godzilla, King of the Monsters! (1956), the American localization of the 1954 film. Originally, the version of Godzilla in the 1954 Japanese film was 50 m tall. In other incarnations of the creature, Godzilla varies in height from 50 m to slightly more than 100 m tall (taller than high-rise buildings and as tall as skyscrapers). He is a prehistoric reptilian or dinosaurian monster that is amphibious or resides partially in the ocean, awakened and empowered after many years by exposure to nuclear radiation and nuclear testing. With the nuclear bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki and the Lucky Dragon 5 incident still fresh in the Japanese consciousness, Godzilla was conceived as a metaphor for nuclear weapons.
