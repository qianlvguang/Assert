**简介**：宠物当家（日文名称：ペット大集合!ポチたま，直译：《宠物大集合！波吉小玉》）是由日本东京电视台制作的宠物节目，于2000年10月20日至2010年3月26日逢星期五晚七点在日本播出，台湾方面则由国兴卫视于星期五晚上七点，香港无线电视J2台在2008年4月至2021年10月29日以《宠物大本营》名字播出。 本节目完结后，松本与大介于2010年4月6日在BS JAPAN频道继续主持另一节目“だいすけ君が行く!!ポチたま新ペットの旅，直译：《大介君出发！波吉小玉新宠物之旅》”（台湾国兴卫视翻译为“宠物当家之旅”），继续到日本各地寻找宠物。

## The Secret Life of Pets · `English Wikipedia`
- **来源**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Secret_Life_of_Pets

**简介**：The Secret Life of Pets is a 2016 American animated comedy film directed by Chris Renaud and written by Brian Lynch, Cinco Paul, and Ken Daurio. Produced by Universal Pictures and Illumination Entertainment, and distributed by Universal, the film stars the voices of Louis C.K., Eric Stonestreet, Jenny Slate, Kevin Hart, Lake Bell, Hannibal Buress, Bobby Moynihan, Dana Carvey, Ellie Kemper, Albert Brooks, and Steve Coogan. Taking place in New York City, the film's plot follows the relationship between a Jack Russell Terrier dog named Max (C.K.) and a giant, unruly dog named Duke (Stonestreet). It also follows what other pets do when their owners are gone away. The Secret Life of Pets premiered at the Annecy International Animated Film Festival on June 16, 2016, and was theatrically released in the United States of America, on July 8 by Universal Pictures. The film received generally positive reviews and grossed $886 million worldwide, making it the sixth-highest-grossing film of 2016 as well as the year's most profitable release. A sequel, The Secret Life of Pets 2, was released on June 7, 2019, with Patton Oswalt taking over the role of Max from Louis C.K.
