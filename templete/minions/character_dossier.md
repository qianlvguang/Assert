## Minions (Despicable Me) · `English Wikipedia`
- **来源**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minions_(Despicable_Me)

**简介**：Minions () are a fictional all-male species of yellow capsule-shaped creatures that appear in Illumination's Despicable Me franchise, created by Eric Guillon, Pierre Coffin, and Chris Renaud. Their first appearance was in Despicable Me (2010), where they serve as the henchmen of the film's protagonist, a supervillain named Gru. The Minions are characterized by their comedic behavior and their fictional language named Minionese, which is largely unintelligible. The Minions play a prominent role in the Despicable Me film tetralogy, additionally starring in their own prequel series, beginning with Minions (2015) and continuing with Minions: The Rise of Gru (2022) and Minions & Monsters (2026). Although they are similar in appearance, several Minions play major roles in the films, such as Kevin, Stuart and Bob. The Minions have also made appearances in short films, an animated web series, Minion Land rides and attractions at Universal Studios Theme Parks, video games and comics.
