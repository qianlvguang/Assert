**简介**：《坏蛋联盟》（英语：The Bad Guys）是一部2022年美国动画抢劫动作喜剧电影，由梦工厂动画公司制作，环球影业发行。该片改编自艾伦·布雷比的同名儿童图书系列，由皮埃尔·佩里费尔执导，伊坦·柯亨和希拉蕊·温斯顿共同编剧，主要配音员包括山姆·洛克威尔、奥卡菲娜、安东尼·拉莫斯、马克·马龙、克雷格·罗宾森、萨琪·毕兹、艾利克斯·布斯汀、理察·艾欧阿德和莉莉·辛格。 《坏蛋联盟》2022年3月16日开始先在国际市场发布，2022年4月22日美国以2D和RealD 3D格式上映，由环球影业发行。电影在影评界获得普遍正面的评价，影评家们主要称赞动画艺术、编剧、幽默风格和配音演出。电影入围第95届奥斯卡金像奖最佳动画片角逐名单，但未进入决选，本片还获得第50届安妮奖五项奖项提名。

## The Bad Guys (film) · `English Wikipedia`
- **来源**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bad_Guys_(film)

**简介**：The Bad Guys is a 2022 American animated neo-noir crime comedy film produced by DreamWorks Animation. Loosely based on the children's book series by Aaron Blabey, the film was directed by Pierre Perifel (in his feature directorial debut) and written by Etan Cohen, while featuring the voices of Sam Rockwell, Marc Maron, Awkwafina, Craig Robinson, Anthony Ramos, Richard Ayoade, Zazie Beetz, Alex Borstein, and Lilly Singh. It tells the story of a criminal group of anthropomorphic animals who, upon being caught, pretend to attempt to reform themselves as model citizens, only for their leader to find himself genuinely drawn to changing his ways for good. Work on the film started in March 2018, with Cohen writing the screenplay. The characters and themes drew inspiration from various crime films and anime series such as Pulp Fiction (1994), the Ocean's film trilogy, Lupin the Third, and Sherlock Hound, while the inspiration for the film's animation style came from Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018).
