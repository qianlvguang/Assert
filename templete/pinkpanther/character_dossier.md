## 粉红豹 · `萌娘百科 (zh)`
- **来源**: https://mzh.moegirl.org.cn/zh-hans/%E7%B2%89%E7%BA%A2%E8%B1%B9

**简介**：粉红豹（英文：Pink Panther），又称顽皮豹，是美国创作人Friz Freleng创作的卡通形象，并有多部衍生作品。

**外貌与造型描述**：粉红豹起初在1963年的真人电影《乌龙帮办》片头中出现，却没料到很受观众欢迎。创作人Friz Freleng就趁热打铁，在翌年推出了一段名为《The Pink Phink》的动画短片，虽然只有7分钟，但借此得以让这只原本默默无闻的卡通形象拥有了自己的名字并开始横行江湖。 粉红豹诞生在美国，以极具喜剧感的主题而著名。其系列电影共计九部，还有动画、游戏等，在NBC电视台推出极受欢迎，成为家喻户晓的卡通影集。另外，粉红豹中让人印象深刻的主题曲，是由亨利·曼西尼 (Henry Mancini) 所作曲，曼西尼算是极成功的电影配乐者，在1958年到1964年之间，几乎主导了美国所有的电视、电影配乐。粉红豹的大紫大红，亨利·曼西尼可说是功不可没。 在中国，粉红豹和多家廉价便利店（比如名创优品）合作，推出周边商品，但粉红豹系列在中国市场上并不怎么受人欢迎。

## Pink Panther (character) · `English Wikipedia`
- **来源**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_Panther_(character)

**简介**：The Pink Panther is a fictional animated character who appears in the opening or closing credit sequences of every film in The Pink Panther series (except for A Shot in the Dark and Inspector Clouseau). In the storyline of the original film, "Pink Panther" is the name of a valuable pink diamond named for a flaw that shows a "figure of a springing panther" when held up to the light in a certain way; in the credits, the diamond is personified by an animated, anthropomorphic panther with pink fur. In the original film series, only the first Pink Panther film and its sequel, The Return of the Pink Panther, featured the diamond. The character's popularity spawned a spin-off franchise of theatrical shorts, television cartoons and merchandise. He starred in 124 short films, four TV series and four TV specials. The character is closely associated with "The Pink Panther Theme", composed by Henry Mancini.
